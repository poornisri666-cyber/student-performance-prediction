How the Prediction System Works

From raw academic inputs to predictive AI insights in four streamlined stages.

1. Input Academic Data

Enter student attendance, internal assessment scores, assignments, study hours, and activity participation.

2. ML Inference

Our trained Machine Learning regression model processes the multi-variable vector to forecast final marks.

3. Risk Classification

Automatically classifies student performance into tiers (Excellent, Good, Average, At Risk) with risk levels.

4. Actionable Guidance

Delivers constructive academic suggestions and logs records into SQLite for institutional dashboard analysis.
